KINGSOK ECOSYSTEM • STRATEGIC BLUEPRINT

AI WhatsApp Real Estate & Lodge Automation System

Autonomous Lead Qualification, Dynamic Video Dispatch, Group Broadcasting & Physical Inspection Conversion for UNN 2026/2027

1. Executive Summary & UNN Market Opportunity

The annual admission of over 6,400+ undergraduate freshers into the University of Nigeria, Nsukka (UNN) creates an immediate, highly concentrated surge in demand for off-campus student accommodation across Nsukka (Hilltop, Odim Gate, Behind Flat, Green House, Ofulonu) and Enugu Campus.

Traditionally, student real estate agencies struggle with manual WhatsApp messaging, leading to slow response times (hours to reply while students message rival agents), tedious manual searches through gallery folders for lodge videos, and missed high-value bookings during peak clearance weeks.

The Solution: "Big Brother KingSok" AI Concierge — a 24/7 autonomous AI system that instantly answers incoming inquiries, qualifies student budgets and location preferences, dispatches matching video walkthroughs and photos, and books physical inspections directly into staff schedules.

2. The 5-Stage Customer Funnel

Stage 1: Inbound Lead Capture
Students message KingSok via group invite links, flyers, or referrals. The AI greets them within 2 seconds by name: "Congratulations on your admission to UNN! 🎉 I'm KingSok's 24/7 Housing Concierge. Let's find your ideal room."
Stage 2: Interactive Qualification
The AI asks 3 structured, friendly questions: Maximum annual budget (e.g. ₦180k, ₦250k, ₦400k), preferred location (Hilltop, Odim, Behind Flat), and required utilities (solar power, borehole water, self-contain vs single room).
Stage 3: Real-Time Catalog Matching
The AI immediately queries the live KingSok Lodge Inventory and filters out fully booked properties, identifying the top 2 matching options.
Stage 4: Dynamic Media Dispatch
The AI automatically sends 3 high-resolution photos, a compressed 15–20 second video walkthrough, exact pricing breakdown, and walking distance to the student's faculty.
Stage 5: Physical Inspection Handoff
The AI asks: "Would you like our KingSok field team to take you on a physical inspection tomorrow at 2:00 PM?" Once confirmed, it triggers an instant staff alert with all details.

3. Automated Community & Daily Group Broadcasting

  • Faculty & Department Communities: 1 WhatsApp Community per Faculty (e.g., Faculty of Pharmaceutical Sciences), with dedicated sub-groups for each Department (Pharmacy, Pharmacology, etc.).
  • Automated Daily "Lodge of the Day" Broadcast: Every morning at 8:30 AM, an automated background scheduler selects a vacant property, generates an engaging, emoji-rich student broadcast caption, and dispatches it to all KingSok groups simultaneously.
  • Anti-Ban Safety Protocols: Employs 3–5 second randomized delays between group posts to protect the WhatsApp account from spam filters and bans.

5. Staff Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

A. Maintaining the Lodge Inventory

Staff members do not need programming knowledge. They simply manage a shared Google Sheet with the following columns: Lodge Name, Location Area, Room Type, Annual Rent, Utilities, Photo Links, Video URL, and Vacancy Status (Available or Occupied).

Critical Rule: As soon as a student pays for a room, staff must change Vacancy Status to Occupied. The AI will instantly stop offering it to new students.

B. Handling Hot Lead Inspection Alerts

When a student confirms an inspection with the AI, the KingSok staff phone receives an automated alert:

🚨 HOT INSPECTION ALERT — KINGSOK ACCOMMODATION
• Student Name: Chidera Okonkwo
• Department: Medical Laboratory Sciences
• Mobile Number: 0803XXXXXXX
• Target Property: Cornerstone Villa (Hilltop) — ₦240k
• Inspection Date & Time: Tomorrow, 2:30 PM
• Assigned Field Guide: Agent Emeka

Staff Action: The assigned field agent must call the student 1 hour prior to confirm arrival at the designated meeting point (e.g., Peace Mass Park or Odim Gate) and conduct the physical inspection.
